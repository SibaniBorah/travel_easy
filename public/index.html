📋 Universal Document Checklist (All Travellers)

Keep these on you at all times — physical copies recommended in remote areas.

✓Aadhaar Card / Passport (original + 2 self-attested copies)
✓ILP / RAP / PAP (if required for the state you are visiting)
✓Passport-size photos (minimum 4 — required for ILP counters)
✓Return ticket / confirmed onward booking
✓Travel insurance policy document (recommended)
✓Offline maps & emergency contacts saved to phone
✓Emergency fund (₹2,000+ cash — ATMs unreliable in remote areas)
✓Medical kit: ORS, altitude pills if going above 3,000m, antihistamines
